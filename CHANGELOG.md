# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

First version, 0.1.0, a research prototype. Experimental canyon validation and a successful fine-grid resolution study remain open gates.

### Added

- Faster native pressure solves, momentum and RANS stencils at unchanged results: a flat IC(0) factor and sweeps, a per-solve compact list of face weights for the PCG products, projection without temporary arrays, shared neighbor lookups and a step that copies only the fields it changes. Paired measurements against the previous implementation (`performance_probe` example, `validation/benchmark_native.py`, frozen cases in `cases/performance/`) show 1.46–2.1× faster pressure solves and 1.36–1.87× faster coupled step batches on two hosts, with bit-identical fields. The memory estimate adds 112 bytes per cell, so cases close to their memory allowance can now be rejected by the preflight check.
- Bounded mapped refinement campaigns (`validation/refinement_campaign.py`): a checked complete coarse snapshot bound to the frozen plan, frozen limits and producer identities, mapping once followed by eligible same-mesh stages, archive checkpoints that bind parents, manifests, sealed summaries and producers, separate coarse and fine work, cancellation-safe verdicts and a conditional two-grid observable comparison. A manual CI selector runs the declared level-2 backstep campaign and retains complete fine archives. Original numerical, audit and measurement gates remain unchanged; two grids do not establish mesh independence or physical qualification.
- One-level coarse-to-fine initialization (`prepare-mapped`): Foundation-13 mapNearest volume fields from an accepted cold source, generated target boundaries, a new-mesh phi, a receipt over the source chain, templates, mapper output and logs, an independent replay of the mapping, and runtime and rebuilt-mesh checks. A mapped and a cold level-2 cavity converge to the same fields; `mapped_start.py` reports mapped and cold level-2 backstep starts. Acceptance thresholds are unchanged.
- Verified same-mesh steady field continuation: source/field/runtime/mesh/control receipts, an independent local-flux check of the source, bounded multi-stage backstep execution with stop reasons, cumulative work and separate final acceptance, and restart comparison tools. A run stopped at its iteration limit keeps its final field; limits that would force near per-iteration writes are rejected. Manual CI runs a fixed-work continuation check and compares a staged reference solve with a single run.
- Wall-resolved external backstep recipes (a graded mesh without cell-size jumps, and SIMPLEC/SIMPLE profiles), an independent Cartesian audit of mesh, local face flux and wall y+, and a gated refinement workflow. The SIMPLEC reference converges and passes the audit on its coarse mesh; no mesh pair is claimed. Solver logs with NaN/Inf values or a diverging continuity error stop execution with a preserved failure archive.
- Bounded OpenFOAM Foundation-13 execution with hashed inputs, a checked input copy, runtime/library identity, a fixed child environment, process-group cancellation, hard address-space and per-file limits, independent stored boundary-flux checks and an archive consistency verifier.
- A cavity comparison between the native and external solvers, a frozen Driver--Seegmiller experimental screening protocol, exact raw-data identities, paired downstream profiles, signed skin-friction/reattachment metrics, trivial-predictor baselines and a first-cell y+ estimate. The measurement screen is assessed only for a numerically accepted solve. These do not qualify the native canyon solver.
- A reproducible non-root installer for the tested OpenFOAM package, external execution tests with a synthetic Foundation-13 runtime, and an optional real-backend CI job that runs the study preset.
- Rust flow and passive-transport library and the `cfd-craft` CLI (describe, check, preview, run, report, verify) with strict case and result schemas.
- `check` diagnostics with codes and field paths, including COST 732 domain and blockage warnings for RANS cases; a catalog in docs/diagnostics.md. Run, restart, output and `report` errors carry codes as well.
- Memory and output estimates that bound the measured peak and the written artifacts, including the monitor history and the per-receptor text; a host-memory refusal that comes before any grid allocation.
- `preview`, which checks the coarsened case and compares its solid volume and fluid topology with the original's cell mask without building the original grid.
- Masked-grid pressure projection with one gauge per closed fluid component and IC(0)-preconditioned CG, checked against an independently assembled reference on a masked grid with an outlet, a sealed pocket and a periodic axis.
- Laminar flow with moving walls, and a neutral k–epsilon package with Richards–Hoxey inflow and rough-wall functions.
- Conservative passive transport (linear upwind or MUSCL/MC with SSPRK2), point, line and volume sources, point and volume receptors, and checked restart.
- Steady acceptance over three successive 100-step monitor windows, with recomputed equation residuals and balances.
- Run archives with a manifest, a completion marker and `report` verification; an independent archived-field checker in Python.
- Analytic and reference verification (periodic shear, Gaussian transport, Poiseuille, Couette, lid cavity, a SciPy sparse reference, an empty-ABL consistency check), source inversion and finite-canyon tutorials.
- A command launcher for optimization-jobs and an optional native fcmaes simulation worker.
